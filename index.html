MIRROR is an original self-reflection experience inspired by ideas from personality psychology (the Big Five / OCEAN framework). It is not a clinical assessment, a diagnostic tool, or a scientifically validated test — treat what it shows you as a starting point for reflection, based on your responses in this experience, not a fixed description of who you are.
